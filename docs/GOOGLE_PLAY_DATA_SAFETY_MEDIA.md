# Google Play Data Safety — Town recordings

Use this when completing the **Data safety** section in Play Console for town podcasts and promo videos.

## Collection in the Android app

The TownTrek app **does not collect** audio or video files for this feature. Town Admins upload MP3s and paste YouTube URLs on the **website** only. The app lists approved items and **streams** playback.

## Displayed / accessed in the app

- **Audio files**: streamed from TownTrek over HTTPS. Users play in-app. There is no save/download control.
- **Videos**: YouTube embeds (video id only). Playback uses YouTube’s player.
- **Photos**: optional cover artwork for the show or episode.

## User-generated content

Posting is restricted to assigned Town Admins and is **reviewed by Towntrek before going live**. Listeners can **Report this recording** (email to support with media id and town name).

## Typical declarations

- **Photos / videos / audio files**: used to display and stream town publisher content. Not collected from the device for this feature.
- **No extra account data** is required to listen or watch.
- **Location**: not used for this feature.

## Retention

Town Admins may keep up to three live audio files and three YouTube videos per town. Removing an item deletes the hosted MP3 from TownTrek storage.
